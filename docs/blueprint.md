# **App Name**: PlenaClin Landing Page

## Core Features:

- Hero Section: Display an emotional image of welcome, a title emphasizing care and well-being ('PlenaClin: Onde o cuidado integral encontra o seu bem-estar'), and a subtitle detailing a comfortable space in Caxias do Sul.
- Space Description: Provide a sensory description of the clinic environment, highlighting comfort, safety, and modernity.
- Specialties Grid: Display a grid of cards for each specialty: Esthetics, Integrative Health (Psychology, Chiropractic, Acupuncture), Performance & Nutrition, and Premium Eyelash Extensions.
- Team Showcase: Introduce 'Our Specialists' with photos, names, and specialties to build authority.
- Dynamic Location Map: Incorporate a dynamic map and detailed address for easy location in Caxias do Sul.
- Floating WhatsApp Button: Implement a floating WhatsApp button with persuasive CTAs like 'Schedule your care experience'.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Sophisticated moss green (#7A9D96) to reflect the brand's identity and luxurious feel, suggesting health and growth.
- Secondary color: Golden beige (#E3DAC9) for subtle details, offering warmth and elegance without overwhelming the moss green.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5F0) for a neutral background, creating a calm and clean look with ample negative space to enhance readability and visual appeal.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif font (similar to Didot), for headlines, because of its high contrast and elegant feel.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif font, for body text, because it will pair well with Playfair and offers a modern yet warm and accessible feel.
- Use minimalist, custom icons for each specialty that align with the 'clean' design and color palette.
- Ensure a clean and airy layout with ample white space to convey tranquility and sophistication, with high-resolution images to maintain a luxury aesthetic.
- Subtle animations to highlight calls to action.
